# OfferZen / Recruiter Screening — Question & Answer Strategy

## 1. "Tell me about yourself and your background."
* **Strategy**: Present a crisp, 90-second professional narrative linking your React foundation, full-stack systems thinking, and AI integration experience.
* **Draft Answer**:
> *"I'm a Front-End Engineer who builds high-performance, accessible web applications in the modern React and TypeScript ecosystem. Over my career, I've moved from component-driven UI development into architecting full-stack monorepos, real-time dashboards, and streaming AI interfaces. Recently, I've focused on combining React frontends with local and cloud-based LLMs—implementing token streaming, conversational state, and offloading heavy AI workloads to Web Workers to keep the UI silky smooth. What excites me about Nutun is the intersection of operational scale and technology—building interfaces that empower thousands of agents and consumers across high-volume customer and credit lifecycles."*

## 2. "Why Nutun specifically?"
* **Draft Answer**:
> *"Nutun isn't just building another generic SaaS product. It's a technology-enabled operational leader handling 18.5 million customer interactions monthly. Their philosophy of 'People Powered by Technology' resonates with me because software here directly impacts real human workflows—whether that's an agent co-pilot reducing call handle time, or a frictionless self-service portal helping distressed consumers rehabilitate their credit. Building resilient, high-speed interfaces for that kind of operational scale is exactly where I want to apply my engineering skills."*

## 3. "Are you comfortable with working in-office 5 days a week in Sandton?"
* **Draft Answer**:
> *"Yes, absolutely. For an operational environment where technologists collaborate directly with operations, agents, and the AI Innovation Lab to solve real-world problems, being in-office alongside the team creates much tighter feedback loops and faster iteration."*
