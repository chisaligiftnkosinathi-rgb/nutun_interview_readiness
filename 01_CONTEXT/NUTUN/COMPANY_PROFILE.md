# Nutun — Company & Business Context

## 1. What Nutun Actually Is
* **Positioning**: Global technology-enabled BPO (Business Process Outsourcing) and Customer/Credit Lifecycle leader headquartered in South Africa (formerly Transaction Capital Limited, JSE: NUT).
* **Operating Volume**:
  * 18.5 million average monthly customer interactions.
  * 24/7/365 operations.
  * Services 2.4 million financially distressed consumers annually.
  * Scales up to 10,000 agents.

## 2. Two Operating Engines
1. **Nutun South Africa**:
   * Collections & recoveries.
   * Acquisition and servicing of Non-Performing Loan (NPL) portfolios.
   * Financial services, specialist lenders, retail credit.
2. **Nutun International**:
   * BPO & customer engagement for UK, US, and Australia.
   * Utilities, retail, telecoms, e-commerce, banking.
   * Regulated governance (UK FCA / SRA, US collections standards).

## 3. The Customer Lifecycle
```text
Acquisition → Onboarding → Servicing → Retention → Early Collections → Late Collections → Legal / Post-Charge-Off → Rehabilitation
```

## 4. Technology Philosophy: "People Powered by Technology"
* Technology does not exist to replace every human; it enables human agents and operational teams to achieve superior business outcomes.
* **Proprietary Platforms**: Cheetah (in-house operational dialler, WFM, CRM), CCaaS (omnichannel telephony & speech analytics), AI Operations Centre, "Zoey" (agentic AI driving collections), and the Durban Innovation Lab (10% international revenue / >R100M investment).
