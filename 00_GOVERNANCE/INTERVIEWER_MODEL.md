# Interviewer Model & Evidence Hierarchy

## 1. Governing Principle
We prepare for the human conversation using verifiable professional context and evidence-based interviewing principles. We strictly avoid armchair psychoanalysis or assuming malicious intent.

```text
KNOWN
  ↓ Publicly verifiable professional information (OfferZen Talent Partner)
OBSERVED
  ↓ Her actual communications with Gift (invitation, messages, specific questions asked)
INFERRED
  ↓ Likely Talent Partner objectives (screening, alignment, communication, narrative)
UNKNOWN
  ↓ Personal interviewing style, unverified background, unannounced questions
```

---

## 2. Two Distinct Interview Modes

### Mode A: Talent Partner / Recruiter Screening (OfferZen — Nozipho)
* **Primary Objective**: Alignment, credibility, career motivation, communication, baseline fit.
* **Format**: Conversational, high-level, narrative-driven.
* **The Funnel**:
  ```text
  "Tell me about yourself"
            │
            ▼
        EXPERIENCE
            │
            ▼
         PROJECTS
            │
            ▼
    TECHNICAL SUMMARY
            │
            ▼
    MOTIVATION & FIT
            │
            ▼
     ALIGNMENT / NEXT
  ```
* **Rule**: Keep answers concise (60–90 seconds), human, and business-focused. Avoid 10-minute technical monologues unless asked to drill down.

### Mode B: Technical Evaluation (Nutun Engineering Team)
* **Primary Objective**: Verifying technical depth, problem-solving, architectural judgement, production engineering standards.
* **The Loop**:
  ```text
  QUESTION → MECHANISM → CODE EVIDENCE → TRADE-OFFS → FAILURE MODES → NUTUN IMPACT
  ```
